# Early In-Person vs Mail Voting 2026, 4 States

Early in-person voters moved toward Democrats in Florida, Kansas, Iowa and North Carolina between 2022 and 2026, as Democrats shifted from mail to early-voting sites.

Published by [Carmena’s Overton & Electoral Research](https://carmenaresearch.com/), an independent, non-partisan US election research firm. These are the public tables behind our charts, from the article [Early in-person voting moved toward Democrats in four states](https://carmenaresearch.com/blog/early-in-person-voting-moves-toward-democrats/). Party means voter registration, not vote choice, unless a column says otherwise. Figures are compared at the same number of days before Election Day in each year.

## Files
- **chart-inperson-margin.csv** (4 rows): Early in-person voters moved toward Democrats in all four states. Columns: `label` State; `margin2022` 2022; `margin2026` 2026; `days` Days before Election Day. Source page: https://carmenaresearch.com/data/#chart-inperson-margin
- **chart-inperson-fl-democrats.csv** (2 rows): Florida Democrats moved from mailboxes to early-voting sites. Columns: `label` Method; `v2022` 2022; `v2026` 2026. Source page: https://carmenaresearch.com/data/#chart-inperson-fl-democrats
- **chart-inperson-mail-share.csv** (4 rows): Mail’s share of the early vote fell in every state. Columns: `label` State; `v2022` 2022 (%); `v2026` 2026 (%). Source page: https://carmenaresearch.com/data/#chart-inperson-mail-share
- **chart-inperson-overall.csv** (4 rows): Counting mail and in-person ballots together, the shift is smaller and Iowa moved the other way. Columns: `label` State; `margin2022` 2022; `margin2026` 2026. Source page: https://carmenaresearch.com/data/#chart-inperson-overall

## Provenance and method
Carmena’s Overton compiles cumulative early-ballot counts from state and county election records and joins them to voter registration and vote history. Calculations are ours; the method is described on our [methodology page](https://carmenaresearch.com/methodology/). Last updated 2026-10-26. Updated as our tracking runs, typically weekly during the early-voting period.

## Caveats
- Days before Election Day: Florida 9, Kansas 8, Iowa 11, North Carolina 11.
- 9 days before Election Day in each year.
- Same days before Election Day as the in-person chart.
